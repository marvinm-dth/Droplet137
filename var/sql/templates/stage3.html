STAGE 3 / 阶段 3 : FRAMING 构架

The foundation is the steel framed trailer that the house is built on top of. It is important that everything on the foundation is correct because this is the starting point of a home. If anything is wrong or missing it could cause serious problems down the line. It is always good to double check things during the building process. Even if you think you have checked something check it again to confirm. If something doesn't look right or you have any questions please talk to a manager before signing your name.

基础是建在钢框架拖车上的房子的根基。确保基础的一切都正确是非常重要的，因为这是房屋的起点。如果现在有任何错误或遗漏 可能会在后续造成严重问题。在建造过程中始终好好检查事物是明智的。即使你认为已经检查过某些东西，也要再次检查以确认 如果有什么看起来不对或者你有任何问题，请在签名前与经理交谈
